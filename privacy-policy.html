DoseOfCare ("we," "us," or "our") publishes educational content about prescription management and pharmacy care. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit doseofcare.com, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few limited ways:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, and message content when you submit our contact form.
  • Automatically collected information — including browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, and referring pages, gathered through standard analytics tools.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small files stored on your device to support basic site functionality and understand how visitors use our content.

We do not knowingly collect health information, medication history, or insurance details through this website. Please do not include sensitive medical information in contact form submissions.

2. How We Use Information

Information we collect is used to:

  • Respond to questions, feedback, or inquiries submitted through our contact form;
  • Understand which articles and topics are most useful to readers;
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the website;
  • Improve the clarity and relevance of future content.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Our site may use cookies or similar technologies to remember basic preferences and measure aggregate traffic patterns. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features but will not prevent you from reading our content.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers who help operate the site (such as hosting or analytics providers), under confidentiality obligations;
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, if required to comply with applicable law or protect our legal rights.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Content on DoseOfCare is provided for general educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a licensed pharmacist, physician, or other qualified healthcare provider regarding any medication or health decision. Nothing on this site should be used to delay seeking professional medical guidance.

6. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain reasonable business records, after which they are securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, contact us using the details below, and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's Privacy

DoseOfCare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our Contact page or email us at hello@doseofcare.com.
